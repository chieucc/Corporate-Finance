## 1. Executive Summary

I recommend FPT Corporation (FPT) for advanced financial analysis as it represents a premier IT services leader successfully pivoting into a globally competitive, "AI-Native" technology innovation company. In 2025, FPT delivered elite financial performance, with profit after tax growing 19.1% to 11,232 billion VND, significantly outpacing its 11.6% revenue growth. This expansion is underpinned by a robust 28.1% Return on Equity (ROE) and a strategic shift toward high-value digital transformation services. The executive should focus on FPT’s structural advantages: its "sovereign AI" ecosystem, featuring NVIDIA-partnered AI Factories ranked 36th and 38th globally among supercomputers, and its vertical integration into semiconductor design. This analysis will test hypotheses regarding operating margin expansion through AI-augmented engineering and valuation re-rating potential as FPT moves from labor-intensive models toward high-margin Managed Services and proprietary IP.

## 2. Background

FPT is transitioning from a service provider to a "technology innovation company" to capture a projected $6.155 trillion global IT spending cycle by 2026. In 2025, the firm achieved double-digit growth despite global volatility, strengthening its role as a critical digital infrastructure enterprise. Driven by an "AI-First" strategy, FPT operates high-performance computing infrastructure specialized for industrial AI production. This analysis evaluates FPT’s ability to maintain high margins while integrating semiconductor design and AI-augmented productivity into core delivery. The scope includes competitive positioning against global legacy leaders like Infosys

## 3. Method

Key frameworks include:
• Financial Modeling: A three-statement historical model (2021–2025) was constructed using audited consolidated financial statements. We applied DuPont Analysis to decompose FPT’s 28.1% ROE, evaluating the relative contributions of net margins, asset turnover, and financial leverage.
• Benchmarking: FPT’s growth and profitability metrics were benchmarked against global peers, specifically Infosys, and industry-wide "save-to-reinvest" trends identified by J.P. Morgan.

Data sources include FPT’s 2024 and 2025 Annual Report. 
Key assumptions include a continued 8.7% CAGR in global IT services spending through 2026 and the successful conversion of FPT’s 1.1 million hours of AI upskilling into a sustained 8.2% productivity premium.

---

## 4. Findings

Three falsifiable, directional hypotheses frame this analysis:

Hypothesis 1: I expect FPT’s net profit margin to expand beyond the 16.0% achieved in FY2025 because comprehensive AI embedding—which already drove an 8.2% productivity increase in its inaugural year — will further decouple revenue growth from linear headcount expansion, particularly within the high-growth Japanese market where revenue grew 25.4% in 2025.

Hypothesis 2: I expect FPT's Return on Equity (ROE) to sustain levels above 25.0% despite the capital intensity of its AI supercomputing infrastructure, because the margin expansion from the proprietary "Made-by-FPT" ecosystem (up 19.1% in 2025) will more than offset any increase in the asset base.

Hypothesis 3: I expect asset turnover to improve from FY2024 to FY2025 because FPT's shift toward Managed Services and software-defined solutions reduces physical asset intensity relative to its double-digit revenue growth.

## 5. Implications

FPT’s transition indicates a structural valuation re-rating opportunity. The 19.1% growth in "Made-by-FPT" revenue demonstrates a successful shift toward high-margin intellectual property. Strategically, FPT is positioned to capture "digital sovereignty" market share through its supercomputing assets, allowing it to compete for large-scale national contracts globally, similar to its record $256 million AI-enabled energy sector deal.
